overview

A hometown salute to a global office

In August 2026 the nominating committee for President of Rotary International named Dr. Bharat Pandya its selection for 2028–29 — carrying a surgeon from Borivli to the head of a service organisation of more than a million members.

Wheel of Honour is the felicitation his own districts are holding in reply. It is a morning of tribute rather than a conference: a formal ceremony from 10:30, remarks from the fellowship that shaped him, and lunch afterwards at Hotel Sahara Star in Vile Parle.

Seating is finite, and attendance is limited to the Council of Governors and to Rotarians of RI Districts 3141 and 3142.

the honouree

Dr. Bharat Pandya

Rotary Club of Borivli, Mumbai
RI District 3141
RI President 2028–29

A general and laparoscopic surgeon for thirty-eight years — and, in parallel, one of the steadiest hands in Rotary’s governance and finances for the better part of two decades.

In Rotary
Past RI Director and Treasurer; member of the Finance Committee and the Strategic Planning Committee.
The Rotary Foundation
Trustee, Vice Chair and Executive Committee Chair; Vice Chair of the Foundation’s Finance and Stewardship Committees.
Polio
Served on the India PolioPlus Committee, 2011–15.
Currently
Chair of the RI Programs Committee and Vice Chair of The Rotary Foundation Programs Committee.
In medicine
Trained at Seth G. S. Medical College and King Edward Memorial Hospital, Mumbai. Fellow of the Association of Surgeons of India and of the International College of Surgeons; founding member of the Association of Minimal Access Surgeons of India.
Recognition
Borivli Medical Brotherhood’s Outstanding Performance Award; the Mumbai Gaurav — Pride of Mumbai — Award.
Family
Madhavi, past district chair of Inner Wheel District 314; two daughters and a granddaughter.

registering

Three minutes, start to finish

  1. 01 Tell us who you are

    Open the registration form and pick your member type — Council of Governors, or Rotarian.

  2. 02 Identify your club

    Governors select their name from the roll. Rotarians select District 3141 or 3142, then their club. Add your name, mobile number and email.

  3. 03 Confirm and pay

    Contribute ₹3,000 online. Your seat is confirmed the moment the payment succeeds, and a confirmation reaches your email.
